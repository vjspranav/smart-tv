G1 · Spread

Like an open magazine. The left page is the index (your apps on home, the four sections in Everything), and the right page shows the selected item: a framed photo with a caption on home, or that section's options as clean rows. Settings change right there with ← →, with no menus to open.

G2 · Headline

Home is one giant word: the app you're on, in huge type in the middle of the screen, with its neighbours fading at the edges. ← → slides between them like flipping headlines, and your show sits underneath as an italic subtitle. In Everything the sections are tabs, and each value is set in huge serif type with its choices listed underneath.

G3 · Columns

A Swiss-style grid of numbered columns, 01 to 05. The selected column widens to show its artwork and your show, while the others stay slim. Everything uses the same grid: four section columns, with the selected one opening up to show its options. Moving ← → feels like widening one column at a time.
