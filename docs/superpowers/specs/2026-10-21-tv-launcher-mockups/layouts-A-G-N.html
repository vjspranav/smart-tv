N · Nothing

Pure black with a faint dot grid, a dot-matrix clock, monospace labels and one red accent. Apps are monochrome pills instead of colourful banners, and "continue" is a widget with a dotted progress bar. "Everything" is a set of widgets: the input switch with its red light, the Spotify record, the TV knobs, and an app drawer.
